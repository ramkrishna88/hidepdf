Guides

These are written for the moment before you attach a PDF to an email. Each one uses the fake sample files on the playground where a concrete file helps, and says what HidePDF Content actually does to that file: render the page, paint over the fields you kept, and rebuild a PDF with no text layer.

Read the one that matches the document in front of you. If you only have time for two, start with why a black box fails, then the checklist for proving a file is really redacted. None of this is legal advice. If a court, a bank, or a regulator has told you how redaction must be done, follow that instruction.

How to redact a PDF properly A full pass over the India demo invoice: what to leave checked, why the rupee total stays visible, and when the download is still the original file. Why black boxes do not hide PDF text A rectangle in Preview changes the picture. The letters underneath are still operators in the file, and copy-paste still works. Removing personal data from a PDF What to strip before you forward an old proposal or contract, and which names the detector will not see. Redacting a scanned PDF with OCR Phone photos and flat scans have no text layer. OCR runs only on sparse pages, and it misreads characters. How to check a PDF is really redacted Search, copy from the box, pdftotext, metadata, and the header this API sets when it refuses an unsafe file. Removing metadata from a PDF Author, title, and old revisions live outside the page. Rasterising drops them. It also drops selectable text you may have wanted to keep. Sharing a bank statement safely What a letting agent actually needs, and why the account number and the closing balance are different decisions. Sharing a payslip safely The US demo pay stub: hide the SSN and the test card, leave gross pay visible unless the money itself is the secret.
