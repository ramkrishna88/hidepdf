Contact

The inbox for this site is hello@hidepdfcontent.com. That is the address for playground questions, API keys, privacy requests, and anything that looks broken. Mail is read by the person who runs hidepdfcontent.com. There is no live chat and no account portal.

hello@hidepdfcontent.com

Send a message

The form does not post to this server. Submit opens a message in your email app, addressed to the inbox above, with the fields already filled in. If your browser blocks that, use the mailto link. Either way, the text stays in your mail program until you send it.

If a mail window did not open, copy your message into an email to hello@hidepdfcontent.com.

What to include

  • Whether you used the playground or the API
  • The time of the request, and any error code, if something failed
  • The page count and a description of the layout, if a field was missed or boxed in the wrong place

Do not email live secrets, card numbers, government IDs, or a customer's full statement. The dummy files on the playground (India dummy and US dummy) are invented. If you need to point at a layout, describe it or use one of those samples.
